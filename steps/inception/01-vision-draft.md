# Step 1 — Vision (first draft)

## Inputs
- The project idea and the domain context provided by the user.
- Active schema instruction for the Vision and its template.
- Existing `Vision and Business Case`, if resuming.

## Output contract
- Brief first draft of the master `Vision and Business Case`.
- Use the headings required by the loaded instruction/template, even in a draft.
- Cover the problem, the business opportunity, the high-level solution idea and the expected value.
- Leave unresolved parts as `TBD` or `pendiente de refinar`.

## Preconditions
- Domain registered and state initialized by step 0.
- Schema instruction and template loaded.

## Rubric
- The draft is a decision aid, not a complete requirements document.
- Headings follow the template; no ad hoc sections.
- The business case is understandable by a non-technical decision maker.

## Evidence required
- Draft in chat.
- List of `TBD` items and open questions.
- `OK 1` request.

## Anti-patterns
- Trying to define all requirements.
- Producing a reliable estimate or project plan.
- Writing files before the batch at step 10.
