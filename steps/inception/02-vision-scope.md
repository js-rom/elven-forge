# Step 2 — Vision (goals and scope)

## Inputs
- The `Vision and Business Case` draft from step 1.
- The same schema instruction and template loaded in step 1.
- User feedback on goals, stakeholders and scope.

## Output contract
- Continue the master `Vision and Business Case` with:
  - stakeholder descriptions and goals;
  - product overview and user environment;
  - in-scope and out-of-scope boundaries;
  - assumptions and business framing;
  - success criteria at business level.
- Keep the same structure and order as step 1.

## Preconditions
- `OK 1` approved.

## Rubric
- Every stakeholder group has a goal and an interest.
- The scope boundary is explicit for both in and out.
- Assumptions are testable or clearly marked as risks.

## Evidence required
- Updated sections in chat.
- Scope in/out list.
- `OK 2` request.

## Anti-patterns
- Defining architecture or technical decisions.
- Listing features instead of goals.
- Leaving scope implicit.
