# Step 9 — Vision (final)

## Inputs
- Current `Vision and Business Case`.
- `Use Case Model`, `Requirements Ranking`, `Supplementary Specification`, `Technical Memo`.
- Active schema instruction and template for the Vision.

## Output contract
- Refined final `Vision and Business Case`, using the same structure as steps 1 and 2.
- Summarize validated information: goals, stakeholders, scope, business case, success criteria and key constraints.
- Resolve the `TBD` items that the previous steps answered; keep only genuine open issues.

## Preconditions
- `OK 8` approved.

## Rubric
- The Vision is consistent with the Use Case Model and the Requirements Ranking.
- No new scope is introduced at this point without being reflected in the ranking.
- The business case is complete enough to decide whether to invest in elaboration.

## Evidence required
- Final draft in chat.
- List of `TBD` items resolved and remaining.
- `OK 9` request before the persistence batch.

## Anti-patterns
- Adding new scope silently.
- Contradicting the approved use case model or ranking.
- Leaving the Vision as a draft when starting elaboration.
