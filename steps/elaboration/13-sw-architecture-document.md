# Step 13 — SW Architecture Document

## Inputs
- Skill `software-architecture-document`.
- `Architectural Analysis`, `Technical Memo`, `Logical View`, `Data Model`, `JPA Mapping`.
- Approved decisions from previous steps.

## Output contract
- `SW Architecture Document` for the iteration: scope and drivers, key decisions and patterns, rationale, 4+1 views, dependency governance, quality scenarios, risks, evolution and traceability.
- It summarizes and links decisions; it does not duplicate diagrams or memo content.

## Preconditions
- `OK 12` approved.
- Decisions and diagrams needed by the views are already approved.

## Rubric
- Every architecturally significant decision in the iteration is represented.
- Quality scenarios have stimulus, response and measure.
- Traceability from drivers to decisions and views is explicit.

## Evidence required
- Section map of the document.
- List of decisions added or revised.
- `OK 13` request.

## Anti-patterns
- Rewriting diagrams instead of referencing them.
- A document without rejected alternatives or consequences.
- Closing the step with open decisions not recorded as risks.
